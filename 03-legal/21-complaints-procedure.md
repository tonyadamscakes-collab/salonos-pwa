# How to complain

**We want to put things right.** Tell us as soon as you can.

## 1. Service and shop complaints
1.1 **Tell us** at the salon, by phone **[PHONE]**, or by email **[EMAIL]**. Please give your name, the date of visit or order number, and what went wrong.
1.2 **What we do:**
- We acknowledge within **[2] working days**.
- A manager (**[NAME]**) looks into it and replies in full within **[10] working days** of the acknowledgement, or tells you when we will.
- We may offer a **redo, repair, replacement, refund or other fix**. *(J. Your rights to a repeat performance or price reduction for services, and to repair, replacement or refund for goods, under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, are not affected (R; ss 54–56 for services (s 54 V), s 22 for goods (V)).)*
1.3 Where a **patch test, allergic reaction or injury** is involved, we will record it and **may ask you to see your doctor**. We tell our insurer **[INSURER]** if needed. **[POLICY TO BE CONFIRMED WITH INSURER]**.
1.4 A record of the complaint, what we did and the result is kept for **[PERIOD]** (LEG-10).

## 2. If you are not satisfied
- **[ADR / OMBUDSMAN – the salon may join a trade body or ADR scheme; **if** it does it must tell customers, and **if** it refuses ADR it must say so (U: Alternative Dispute Resolution for Consumer Disputes (Competent Authorities and Information) Regulations 2015; adviser)]**: **[SCHEME NAME AND CONTACT]**.
- You can also contact **Citizens Advice** (consumer helpline) **[R]**: https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/consumer/.
- Court action remains available.

## 3. Data protection complaints
3.1 Contact our privacy contact **[PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]** (see the privacy notice, LEG-05). We aim to acknowledge within **30 days** (R) and reply without undue delay.
3.2 You may also complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office**: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/ , telephone **0303 123 1113** (R, check). *(You do not have to complain to us first; a data protection complaint to the ICO is separate (R).)*

## 4. Discrimination or accessibility
Tell us if you think you were treated unfairly because of a protected characteristic, or about an accessibility problem (LEG-19). We take it seriously. You can also contact the **Equality Advisory and Support Service** (R).

## 5. Staff concerns
Staff complaints follow our grievance procedure **[LINK]**, not this page.

## 6. Learning
Complaints are logged, reviewed **[monthly]** and used to improve services. No unfair treatment follows a complaint.
