## 7. Products, health and patch tests
7.1 The salon uses **its own professional products** (brands as stated in the quote **[BRANDS]**). If the client wants a particular product, we must agree it in advance and we may decline it.
7.2 Everyone treated must give true health and allergy information on the consultation form (LEG-09) **before** the date. A patch test is needed for services that require one (hair colour: at least 48 hours before; NHBF, R). **We will not go ahead without a valid test unless a manager has recorded that the client was told the risk, chose to decline the test, and that the service may not be covered by insurance (LEG-01 clause 6.3, LEG-09 Part 4).**
7.3 Under-16s: a parent or guardian must be present and sign (LEG-01 clause 7). The salon checks each product's manufacturer instructions first.

## 8. Cancellation and changes by the client
8.1 **[CANCELLATION SCHEDULE – salon sets; adviser to review for fairness. Example structure only: more than [NUMBER] days before: deposit [kept / transferred to a new date]; between [NUMBER] and [NUMBER] days: [%] of the quote; less than [NUMBER] days or no-show: [%] of the quote.]**
8.2 Any sum we keep or charge must reflect our **real loss** (we cannot re-sell the date, we have turned other work away, we have booked staff). A term that is out of proportion may not be binding on the client (Consumer Rights Act 2015 s 62, V). Stage and balance payments already due stay due. **The "greater of X% or £Y" deposit and the schedule above are both editable by the salon. A charge above the deposit already taken exists here only because this signed agreement states it; it is not taken from a card without a second confirmation and a recorded reason (TIL-13).**
8.3 **Statutory cancellation right.** A booking made online or by phone is a distance contract. The client may have a 14-day right to cancel unless an exception applies. **Whether the "services related to leisure activities" exception (CCR reg 28(1)(h)) covers a booking for a specific date is an adviser question (OI-05); this clause does not rely on it.** If the client asks us to start before the 14 days end, and cancels, they pay for what has been done, and a date within 14 days is handled as the CCR require (reg 36, V). **[Adviser to confirm wording.]**
