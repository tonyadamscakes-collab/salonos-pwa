## 1. Rules the wording follows
- **PECR reg 22(2) (V):** unsolicited marketing by electronic mail (email and SMS) needs the recipient's **prior consent**, unless the **soft opt-in** in reg 22(3) applies.
- **Soft opt-in, reg 22(3) (V):** all three must be true: (a) we got the contact details **in the course of a sale or negotiations for a sale**; (b) the marketing is of **our own similar** products or services; (c) the person was **given a simple, free way to refuse** when the details were collected, **and in every later message**.
- **Spec D19 (working default): soft opt-in is OFF.** Every marketing channel is opt-in, unticked, unless the salon switches soft opt-in on for **email to existing clients** (LYC-07).
- **UK GDPR consent** must be freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous, separate from other terms, and as easy to withdraw as to give (U: Art 4(11), 7).
- **Joining the Club or booking never requires marketing consent** (LYC-01).
- **Health data is never used** to choose recipients (LYC-05).
- **Web push** needs the browser permission and the member's consent (J).
- **Service messages** (confirmations, reminders, order updates, patch-test reminders) are not marketing and do not need this consent (MSG-01).

## 2. Sign-up wording (three separate unticked boxes)
> **Would you like to hear from [SALON NAME]?** You can choose each separately, say no to all, and change your mind at any time. Booking or joining the Club does not depend on your answer.
>
> ☐ **Email** me about offers, new treatments, new products and deals from [SALON NAME].
> ☐ **Text message (SMS)** me about offers and deals from [SALON NAME]. *(Only if the salon has switched SMS on. Your network may charge.)*
> ☐ **Push notifications** to this device about offers and deals from [SALON NAME].
>
> **Topics** (choose any): ☐ promotions ☐ new treatments ☐ new products ☐ deals.
>
> We will never use your health information, allergy or patch-test records to decide what we send you. We use your details as in our privacy notice [LINK LEG-05]. You can **unsubscribe** with one click in any email, by replying **STOP** to any text, by turning off notifications in your browser, or in your member page. Contact **[SALON EMAIL]**.

*Record on each tick (CMP-02):* client, channel, topic, wording version, date and time, source (booking page, till tablet, Club page, paper form).

## 3. In-salon / till tablet or paper wording
Same three unticked boxes, with the line: "I understand I can withdraw consent at any time." A staff member must **not tick on the client's behalf**. If a paper form is used, the staff member enters it and attaches the scan.

## 4. Soft opt-in wording (only if the salon switches it on; email only)
At the point of sale or booking: "We will email you about similar services and products from [SALON NAME]. You can **opt out now** [☐ do not email me offers] **and in every email**."
*The setting text must show the three reg 22(3) conditions (LYC-07).*

## 5. Footer for every marketing email
"You are receiving this because [REASON, e.g. you joined the [SALON NAME] Club]. **Unsubscribe** [LINK]. [SALON LEGAL NAME], [ADDRESS]. Sent using software supplied by [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME]." *(Sender identity and a valid opt-out address are required for marketing email by PECR reg 23 (U).)*

## 6. SMS format
"[SALON NAME]: [MESSAGE]. Reply STOP to opt out." Opt-out takes effect **immediately** and is honoured on every channel it covers (MSG-05).

## 7. Withdrawal and suppression
An unsubscribe stops the next send. We keep a **suppression list** (contact and the fact of opt-out) so we do not message again, even after erasure of other data (J: lawful under legitimate interest to honour the objection).

## 8. Telephone marketing and WhatsApp
Out of scope for v1. Telephone marketing is regulated by PECR reg 21 and the TPS (U); WhatsApp marketing needs the recipient's consent and the provider's rules **[ADVISER if the salon wishes to market on WhatsApp]**.

## 9. Preview and testing
Nothing is sent from the preview environment except to named test addresses (LYC-11).
