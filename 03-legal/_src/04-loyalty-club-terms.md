3.1 You can exchange points for rewards **[REWARD TABLE – salon sets; no value is set by SalonOS]**. Rewards are shown in your member page and at the till.

Member-only offers are shown when you are signed in. Offers are limited by their stated dates and conditions and cannot be combined unless we say so. ## 5. Your data
Your details are used to run the Club (points, rewards, messages you asked for). See LEG-05. Health information (allergy and patch test records) is **never used to select who receives offers**.

## 6. Leaving
You can leave at any time from your member page or by telling us. Your points then end. We keep records we must keep by law (LEG-10).
