*Public bodies must publish an accessibility statement under the Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations 2018. A private salon is **not** required to by those regulations (U), but publishing one is good practice and supports the **Equality Act 2010 s 29 duty not to discriminate in providing a service** and the **s 29(7) reasonable-adjustments duty** (V). The statement below is a draft for the salon to complete after testing.*
