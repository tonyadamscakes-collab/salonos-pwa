## 3. Using a voucher
3.1 Show or tell us the code at payment, on any visit. You can use all or part of the value. **The balance stays on the voucher.** We tell you the balance on request.
3.2 A voucher is not exchangeable for cash, and we do not give change. If the bill is more than the voucher, you pay the rest. The voucher cannot go below zero.
3.3 You may give or sell a voucher to someone else. We treat the holder of a valid code as entitled to use it, **unless we have been told it is lost or stolen** and have blocked it.
3.4 Redeeming a voucher does not earn loyalty points on the voucher amount when you **buy** it; points are earned on completed paid services and products as the loyalty terms say (working default, Tony to confirm). **[LOYALTY HANDLING – Tony to confirm]**
