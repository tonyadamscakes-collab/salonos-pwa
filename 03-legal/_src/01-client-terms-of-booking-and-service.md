## 5. Your right to cancel an online booking (distance contracts)
5.1 If you book online or by phone, the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 (CCR) may give you a **14-day right to cancel** a service contract without giving a reason. For a service, the 14 days run from the day the contract is made (reg 29(1), reg 30(2)) (V).
5.2 We will not start your service within those 14 days unless you make an **express request** for us to do so (reg 36(1)) (V). You can make it by ticking the box on the booking screen. If you then cancel within the 14 days after we have started, you must pay us an amount in proportion to what we have already provided, calculated on the agreed price (reg 36(4) and (5)) (V).
5.3 If we have **fully performed** the service, after your express request and with your acknowledgement that you would lose the right to cancel once it is fully performed, the right to cancel ends (reg 36(2)) (V). The booking screen shows that acknowledgement before you confirm.
5.4 The CCR do not apply to a contract for "services related to leisure activities" if it provides for a specific date or period of performance (reg 28(1)(h)) (V for the text). **Whether a beauty or hair appointment counts as a leisure activity is an open legal question (J, OI-05).** We therefore do not rely on that exception without adviser sign-off, show our own cancellation and deposit terms (clause 4) before you book, and ask for the express request in 5.2. A booking made in person at the salon is not a distance contract (J).
5.5 Model cancellation wording: see **Document LEG-02**, annex.

## 6. Patch tests, consultation and health information
6.1 Some services (for example hair colour, some lash, brow and tint services) need a **patch test** first. We will tell you when you book. The usual lead time for hair colour is **at least 48 hours before the appointment** (NHBF allergy alert testing, R); for some beauty skin sensitivity tests it is **at least 24 hours** (NHBF code of practice, R). The salon's own time and manufacturer instructions apply: **[PATCH TEST LEAD TIME – salon sets per treatment]**.
6.2 A test is valid for **[PATCH TEST VALIDITY – salon sets]**. If it has expired, we will ask you to take a new one.
6.3 If you do not have a valid test we may **refuse to do the service** or move you to a patch test appointment. We will not do a patch-test service without a valid test unless a **manager** has recorded, on your consultation form (LEG-09, Part 4), **all three of these**: (a) that you were told the risk of a severe allergic reaction, (b) that you chose to decline the test, and (c) that the service **may not be covered by insurance**. We may still refuse. **[DECLINED-TEST POLICY – salon sets whether it ever allows this.]** *(PTT-02, PTT-06; Tony 23:58, fine-tune 2.)*
6.3A **Note for the salon (not for display):** some insurers refuse cover for a reaction after a declined test, or require a test for named products. **The salon must check its own policy wording and confirm with its insurer before it allows any declined test (J, adviser item).** The manager record is the salon's evidence that the client was warned; it is not a promise that insurance will pay.
6.4 If you have ever reacted to a product or have a skin or health condition, medicine, or pregnancy that could affect the service, you must tell us **before** the service. We will ask you to complete a consultation and health questionnaire (Document LEG-09). If you do not tell us, or the information is wrong, we may refuse or stop the service and we are not responsible for a reaction we could not have known about. This does not limit your rights under the law (see clause 10).
6.5 If you have had a reaction, we will not use that product again for you until the owner has cleared it. *(PTT-04.)*
6.6 We store health information securely and use it only to keep you safe. See our client privacy notice (LEG-05).

10.2 Goods we sell you must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described (CRA 2015, Part 1 Chapter 2) (U for exact section numbers). You normally have a **short-term right to reject of 30 days** for faulty goods (s 22) (V).
10.3 Nothing in these terms limits any right you have by law that cannot be limited. *(CRA 2015 s 31 and s 57 prevent exclusion of those rights (U).)*
10.4 If you are unhappy with a service, tell us as soon as possible, ideally the same day, so we can put it right. See our complaints procedure (LEG-21).
