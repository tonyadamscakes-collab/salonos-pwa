*Basic template (approved scope: "rent-a-chair basic"). The salon is responsible for deciding whether a person is genuinely self-employed. **SalonOS does not decide employment status** (STF-06, RNT-06). **Employment status, VAT and licence-vs-tenancy are adviser items (OI-04).***

**THIS AGREEMENT** is made on **[DATE]** between:
(1) **[SALON LEGAL NAME]**, company number **[COMPANY NO]**, of **[ADDRESS]** (the **"Salon"**); and
(2) **[RENTER NAME]**, trading as **[RENTER BUSINESS NAME]**, of **[RENTER ADDRESS]**, UTR **[UTR]**, VAT number (if any) **[RENTER VAT NUMBER]** (the **"Renter"**).

## 1. Licence to use a chair (not a tenancy)
1.1 The Salon grants the Renter a **non-exclusive licence** to use the **[CHAIR / STATION / ROOM]** and shared facilities at **[ADDRESS]** during **[AGREED HOURS]**. It is **not** a lease and gives the Renter no exclusive possession of any part of the premises. *(J: licence vs tenancy depends on the facts, including the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 (U). **Adviser to confirm.**)*
1.2 The Salon may move the Renter to another station on reasonable notice.

## 2. Fees and settlement (choose ONE rule; spec RNT-01)
- ☐ **Option A. Percentage split.** The Salon keeps **[COMMISSION %]** and the Renter keeps the rest of each sale attributed to the Renter, **before / after** card costs **[SALON TO CHOOSE]**.
- ☐ **Option B. Fixed rent.** The Renter pays rent of **[RENT AMOUNT – Tony/salon to set]** per **[WEEK / MONTH]**, due **[DUE DAY]**, and keeps the rest of their sales.
- ☐ **Option C. Record only.** SalonOS records the Renter's sales for information; money is settled outside the system.

2.1 **Settlement.** The Salon gives the Renter a statement for each period showing sales, split, tips (clause 6), amounts owed and rent due. Amounts owed to the Renter are paid on **[PAYOUT DAY]** by **[METHOD]**. **Payouts are made by the Salon outside SalonOS; SalonOS does not pay out in v1 (RNT-04).**
2.2 The Renter checks each statement and tells the Salon of any error within **[DAYS]** days.
2.3 Late payment: **[SALON SETS; must be reasonable]**.

## 3. Who sells to the client, and VAT
3.1 **[SUPPLIER OF RECORD – adviser to confirm: Renter supplies the service to the client in their own name, or the Salon makes the supply and pays the Renter.]** The receipt and booking pages must make this clear to the client.
3.2 Each party is responsible for its own VAT and tax. **[OPERATOR VAT POSITION]**, **[VAT NUMBER]**. The Renter must register for VAT if required.
3.3 **Adviser item (OI-04):** HMRC treatment of chair rental and who makes the supply differs by case (R: HMRC hair and beauty guidance). No tax advice is given here.

## 4. Self-employed status
4.1 The parties intend the Renter to be **self-employed**, in business on their own account. The Renter sets their own prices within **[PRICE POLICY – note: controlling prices may point to employment; adviser]**, chooses how to carry out work, supplies their own tools and **may send a suitably qualified substitute** with the Salon's reasonable approval **[SALON TO CONFIRM; a right of substitution is a status factor]**.
4.2 The Renter is not entitled to holiday pay, sick pay or a pension from the Salon, and is responsible for their own tax and National Insurance.
4.3 **Warning (RNT-06):** HMRC and tribunals look at control, financial risk, substitution, mutuality of obligation and who supplies products. If the facts show employment, a label in this agreement will not change that. The Salon bears the risk of a wrong decision. *(J; R: HMRC guidance.)*

## 5. Clients, bookings and data
5.1 Clients booked into the Renter's column are booked through the Salon's SalonOS. **[CLIENT OWNERSHIP – salon sets: whether clients are the Salon's, the Renter's or shared.]**
5.2 Client personal data: **[CONTROLLER POSITION – adviser: the Salon is controller of the shared client record; the Renter may be a separate controller for clients they bring. See LEG-12.]** The Renter must follow the Salon's privacy and consent rules, may not export the client list, and sees **only their own** sales and clients' relevant records (ROL-07).
5.3 The Renter must complete a consultation and patch test before every service that needs one, using the Salon's forms (LEG-09), and record them in SalonOS.

## 6. Tips
6.1 A tip to the Renter is recorded and attributed to the Renter on their statement and is passed to the Renter in full. Whether a Renter's tip is a **qualifying tip under Part 8A of the Employment Rights Act 1996** depends on whether the Renter is a "worker"; **SalonOS does not decide** (TIP-08). **[ADVISER – OI-12]**
6.2 Tips given directly by a client to the Renter in cash belong to the Renter.

## 7. Insurance, qualifications, standards
7.1 The Renter must hold **public liability and professional indemnity insurance** of at least **[AMOUNT]**, and give the Salon a copy **[DATE]**; qualifications **[LIST]**; compliance with health and safety, COSHH, hygiene and patch-test rules.
7.2 The Renter uses their own products, or the Salon's products at **[PRICE – to be set]**. Hair formula records are kept on the client card.

## 8. Premises and conduct
Opening hours, cleaning, noise, conduct, use of the Salon name and brand **[SALON SETS]**. The Renter must not solicit the Salon's employees' clients using Salon data.

## 9. Liability and indemnity
Each party is responsible for its own acts. The Renter indemnifies the Salon for claims arising from the Renter's services **[SOLICITOR TO DRAFT]**. Nothing excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or fraud.

## 10. Term and ending
10.1 Starts **[DATE]**. **[FIXED TERM or ROLLING]**. Either party may end it on **[NOTICE PERIOD]** written notice; the Salon may end it immediately for serious breach (for example lack of insurance, unsafe practice, theft).
10.2 On ending: settle accounts, return keys and equipment, SalonOS access ends, and the Renter's SalonOS record is retained as LEG-10 requires.
10.3 **[RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS – solicitor; keep reasonable or omit]**

## 11. General
Entire agreement; variation in writing; no partnership or agency; governing law England and Wales **[JURISDICTION]**; counterpart signing.
