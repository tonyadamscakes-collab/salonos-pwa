*Employers must pass on all qualifying tips, gratuities and service charges to workers **without deductions** and must have a **written tipping policy** and keep **records for three years**. The law is Part 8A of the **Employment Rights Act 1996**, inserted by the **Employment (Allocation of Tips) Act 2023** (in force 1 October 2024) and the statutory **Code of Practice on fair and transparent distribution of tips**. The salon fills the bracketed choices. SalonOS stores the policy text and version (TIP-04).*

## 1. What this policy covers
1.1 It covers every **tip, gratuity and service charge** paid by clients to **[SALON NAME]** by **card in the salon, card online (if available), external card machine, or cash handed over at the till**.
1.2 It applies to our **employees and workers**. Self-employed stylists and chair renters: **[SALON STATEMENT – whether they are "workers" is decided on the facts; see LEG-14 clause 6, LEG-15 clause 4; SalonOS does not decide]**.

## 2. Principles
2.1 **Tips belong to staff, not the Salon.** They are held as money owed to staff ("tips payable") and are **never** counted as Salon income (TIP-02).
2.2 **Full pass-through.** The amount allocated to staff **equals the amount of tips received**. **We make no deduction**, including for card processing fees, by default (TIP-03). *(V: the Act makes it mandatory for all tips to be passed on to workers without deductions (GOV.UK page, 29 July 2026 update). **Open point OI-12:** whether any deduction for card-payment costs is allowed under the Code has **not been verified**; v1 default is no deductions; adviser to confirm.)*
2.3 We pass tips on **no later than the end of the month following the month the client paid** (ERA 1996 s 27G) (V).

- ☐ **Named recipient:** a tip is given to the staff member the client names or who served them. **[RULE]**
- ☐ **Shared pool ("tronc")** among **[GROUPS: stylists / assistants / reception]**, by **[FAIR RULE – hours worked / points / equal]**. A tronc and any independent tronc master must be fair and transparent. **[RULE TO BE WRITTEN BY THE SALON; document how decisions are made]**
- ☐ **Mixed:** **[RULE]**
Each allocation in SalonOS records **the rule used** (TIP-04). The rule applies to **all** tips fairly; it must not favour or penalise staff for protected characteristics or for ordinary workplace disputes.

## 4. Cash tips handed directly to staff
Cash a client gives **directly to a staff member** is not an employer tip and is not part of the payable total. It may be logged for information and is labelled so (TIP-07). *(U: how tax applies; adviser.)*

## 5. How and when you are paid
Tips are paid with **[PAYROLL / SEPARATE PAYMENT]** on **[DATE EACH MONTH]**. A **monthly tips report** shows tips received, allocated and paid, and the date paid (TIP-05). Payment is recorded by the owner; SalonOS does not make payments.

## 6. Records and your right to see them
6.1 We keep tips records for **three years** (ERA 1996 s 27J) (V). SalonOS prevents deletion inside three years (TIP-06).
6.2 You may **ask in writing for the records of tips** received and allocated to you or your group (statutory right: U for section number; Acas guidance says the employer should respond within four weeks (R)).
