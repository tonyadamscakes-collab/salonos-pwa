## 1. Who we are
**[SALON LEGAL NAME]**, **[ADDRESS]** ("we") is the controller of the personal data of people who work with us: employees, workers, apprentices, self-employed stylists and chair renters, contractors and job applicants ("you"). ICO registration **[ICO REG NO]**. Privacy contact **[SALON EMAIL]**. **[OPERATOR LEGAL NAME]** provides our software as processor.

## 2. What we hold
- Contact and identity: name, address, date of birth, national insurance number (employees), photo and bio (staff profile).
- Contract data: staff type (employed or self-employed), tier, hours, rota, holiday and absence, commission settings.
- Pay data: hours, pay, commission, tips received and allocated, rent due and settlements for renters, bank details **[PAYROLL PROVIDER]**.
- Performance and diary data: bookings, takings, retail sales, average ticket, no-shows (the performance screen).
- Login and audit data: each login, actions on client cards, voids, refunds, cash drawer opens, role changes (audit log).
- Right-to-work documents and training / qualifications **[SALON TO CONFIRM]**.
- Absence and health information **only** where needed for sick pay or adjustments.
We do **not** use biometrics, CCTV analytics or location tracking in SalonOS.

## 3. Why and on what basis
| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Employ you or contract with you, run the rota, pay you, tips, commission, rent | **Contract** (Art 6(1)(b)) |
| Tax, National Insurance, tips records, right to work, health and safety | **Legal obligation** (6(1)(c)) |
| Keep the salon secure, prevent fraud, investigate misuse (audit log) | **Legitimate interests** (6(1)(f)) |
| Health or sickness information | Art 9(2)(b) employment, social security and social protection law, with a DPA 2018 Sch 1 condition |

## 4. Tips
We record every tip taken (card, external card, cash) as money held for staff, not salon income, and pass **all** of it to staff with no deductions, under our written tips policy (LEG-16). We keep tip records for **three years** (Employment Rights Act 1996 s 27J, inserted by the Employment (Allocation of Tips) Act 2023 (V)). You can ask for the record of tips paid to you; see LEG-16 for timings.

## 5. Who sees your data
Owners and managers (as their role allows), our payroll provider **[PAYROLL PROVIDER]**, accountant, HMRC, pension provider, insurer. Stylists see only their own column and takings; **renters see only their own sales and split**. The software supplier does not see staff data unless it is given logged, time-limited support access.

## 6. How long we keep it
Payroll records for **[PAYROLL RETENTION – adviser; HMRC requires at least 3 years after the end of the tax year (R)]**. Tips records 3 years. Absence and personnel records **[PERIOD]** after you leave. Audit logs **[PERIOD]**. See LEG-10.

## 7. Your rights
Access, correction, erasure (where it applies), restriction, objection and, where relevant, portability. We cannot erase data we must keep by law. Complaints: us first (LEG-21), then the ICO.

## 8. Self-employed and renters
Whether you are employed or self-employed is decided by the facts of how you work and is the salon's legal responsibility, **not a setting in SalonOS** (J: STF-06, RNT-06). If you are self-employed, we hold your business details, UTR if you give it, and settlement records, and we may share them with HMRC where the law requires.
