## 3. Placing an order
3.1 When you place an order you make an offer to buy. We accept it by sending an **order confirmation email**; the contract is made then. We may refuse an order (for example a pricing error, or product out of stock) and will refund anything you have paid. *(J: salon to confirm acceptance mechanics.)*
3.2 You pay by card through Stripe, or by other methods we show at checkout **[PAYMENT METHODS]**. The order confirmation shows what you paid, including delivery.
3.3 If a product, price or availability is wrong, we will tell you before we accept. We do not hold stock for you until we confirm.
3.4 Orders from signed-in members earn loyalty points under the Club terms (LEG-04), at the time the salon sets (spec default: when dispatched, reversed on refund). *(ORT-20, LOY-09.)*

## 4. Delivery
4.1 We deliver to **[DELIVERY AREA – salon sets, e.g. UK mainland]**. Delivery charges and timings are shown at checkout **[DELIVERY PRICES AND TIMES – salon sets from its own courier rates]**. No courier price is built in. *(Courier prices change; we cannot verify them, section 8.7 of the research.)*
4.2 Unless we agree otherwise, we deliver within **[DELIVERY TIME, not more than 30 days]** of your order *(Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 reg 40: default 30 days (U; adviser to confirm))*.

## 5. Click and collect

5.2 Bring your order email or number and photo ID if we ask. We hand over the goods to you or to a person you name, and record the collection.
5.3 The cancellation period for click and collect runs from the day the goods come into your possession or the possession of a person you identify to take them (CCR reg 30(3)) (V).
5.4 If you do not collect, we may cancel and refund after the hold period **[POLICY – salon sets]**.

## 6. Your right to cancel (14 days)
6.1 You have the right to cancel this contract **within 14 days without giving any reason**. The period ends **14 days after the day on which you, or someone you identify (not the carrier), take physical possession of the goods**. For an order delivered in several parts, it is 14 days after the last part arrives (CCR reg 29, reg 30(3)–(5)) (V).
6.2 To cancel, tell us clearly by letter, email or the form in the Annex, or press the "Cancel this order" button in your account. You can use the model form but you do not have to.
6.3 If you cancel, we will refund **all payments received, including standard delivery**. If you chose a more expensive delivery than our least expensive common delivery, we refund only up to the standard price (reg 34(1)–(3)) (V). We refund within **14 days after we receive the goods back, or after you send proof you have sent them back, if earlier** (reg 34(5)) (V). If we offer to collect the goods, the 14 days run from the day you tell us you are cancelling (reg 34(6)) (V). We refund to the card or means you used, with no fee (reg 34(7), (8)) (V).
6.4 **Exceptions: products you cannot cancel.**
 - **Sealed hygiene products** that you have **unsealed after delivery** (opened cosmetics, for example) (CCR reg 28(3)(a)) (V). The exception applies **only if the seal was broken after delivery**. If a product arrives **already unsealed, or damaged, or not as described**, you keep your rights (see clause 9).
 - Goods **made to your specification or clearly personalised** (reg 28(1)(b)) (V). **[SALON TO LIST ANY SUCH ITEM; none by default]**
 - Goods that **expire or deteriorate rapidly** (reg 28(1)(c)) (V). **[SALON TO LIST ANY SUCH ITEM]**
 - Goods that, after delivery, are by their nature **inseparably mixed** with other items (reg 28(3)(c)) (V), for example a **custom-mixed hair colour**.
 The checkout shows these notices **before** you pay, product by product (ORT-16).
 *(The salon decides which products carry a flag. We do not decide that a product is unsuitable for return. J.)*

## 7. Returns
7.1 If you cancel, **send the goods back to us without delay and in any event within 14 days** of telling us you are cancelling. Send them to **[RETURN ADDRESS]** quoting the return reference we give you. *(Reg 35 states the 14-day return window; U for the exact wording; adviser to check.)*
7.2 **Return postage:** **you pay** the direct cost of returning the goods **unless** we say otherwise below. **[SALON CHOICE – "We pay return postage" or "You pay return postage". Must be stated before the order. Schedule 2(m).]**
7.3 Take reasonable care of the goods while they are with you. If their value has been reduced by handling **beyond what is needed to establish their nature, characteristics and functioning** (more than you could do in a shop), we may deduct that from the refund (reg 34(9), (12)) (V). We will not make a deduction if we did not give you the correct information about the right to cancel (reg 34(11)) (V).
7.4 When we receive your return, staff check it and note the result, then refund (ORT-17). Items that cannot be resold are written off.

## 9. Faulty, damaged or wrong goods (not limited by the 14-day right)
9.1 Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 goods must be of **satisfactory quality, fit for their purpose and as described**. If they are not, you may have the right to **reject them within 30 days** (the short-term right to reject: the time limit is 30 days from the day after ownership has passed, delivery has happened and installation, if needed, is complete (CRA 2015 s 22(3)) (V)), a repair or replacement, or a price reduction or final right to reject, depending on when you tell us (CRA 2015 ss 19–24) (U for the sections other than s 22).
9.2 Contact us with your order number and a photo. We will tell you what happens next and pay reasonable return costs for faulty goods.
9.3 Nothing in these terms limits your statutory rights.
